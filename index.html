BRAND REFRESH

Helping an established business rediscover its identity, and its confidence.

The challenge

A business owner had grown uncomfortable with the name and identity he had chosen when starting his business back in 2002. Although the business had evolved over the years, the branding had not kept pace.

My approach

Rather than starting again and losing the history behind the business, I refreshed its identity and presentation to better reflect the business it had become.

The outcome

The owner felt proud of his brand again, with renewed confidence in how he presented it. He reported an immediate increase in bookings once the new branding was applied to his website.

SOCIAL MEDIA & EMAIL

Back on the local radar.

Experienced, flexible marketing support that made the business visible again.

Marketing strategyCustomer engagementRegular enquiries
Read the story

The challenge

An established local business recognised that its marketing needed more attention and was considering employing a junior marketing team member. What it needed was experienced support to identify opportunities, build a clear approach and deliver the work, without the commitment and overheads of recruitment.

My approach

I provided flexible, hands-on marketing support, combining planning with day-to-day delivery. I developed a more consistent approach to social media and customer communications, with regular content, useful advice and opportunities for community engagement. The focus was on visibility, relationships and enquiries rather than simply posting more.

The outcome

The business gained access to experienced marketing support at a lower cost than recruiting an employee, alongside a more consistent presence, stronger community engagement and regular new business enquiries.

WEBSITE & LOCAL SEARCH

A place to be found.

A welcoming online home that makes it easier for local customers to get in touch.

Website buildLocal SEO foundationsClear enquiry journey

The challenge

An independent professional needed a straightforward, credible online presence where potential customers could understand the services available, find relevant information and make contact.

My approach

I created a simple, mobile-friendly website focused on what customers actually need, including clear service descriptions, local area information, straightforward enquiry options and foundational local search optimisation.

The outcome

The business now has a dedicated online home providing a clearer route from discovering the service to making an enquiry. Search visibility and longer-term performance are still to be measured.
