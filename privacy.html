YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Last updated: 4 November 2026

I'm Tiffany Bale, an independent marketing consultant trading as Tiffany Bale Marketing, based in Devon, UK. I respect your privacy and aim to be clear about how I handle personal information when you visit my website or get in touch.

Who is responsible for your information?

Tiffany Bale, trading as Tiffany Bale Marketing, is responsible for deciding how personal information submitted through this website is used (the data controller). For questions or requests about your information, email tiffanybale@hotmail.co.uk.

What information I collect

If you complete my enquiry form, I collect your name, email address and message. You can also choose to provide your business name and the type of marketing support you're interested in. If you contact me directly, I may keep the details you include in your correspondence.

I ask you not to include sensitive personal information in your enquiry unless it's genuinely necessary.

Why I use your information

I use enquiry information to respond to your message, discuss your requirements and, if appropriate, prepare a proposal or take steps towards providing services.

My lawful basis is generally legitimate interests in answering enquiries and managing potential client relationships. Where you ask me to take steps towards entering a contract with you, I may also rely on steps taken at your request before entering a contract. I use only the information reasonably needed for these purposes.

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails. I won't add you to a promotional mailing list simply because you contacted me.

How your enquiry is handled

My website uses Formspree to process enquiry form submissions and deliver them to me. Formspree processes information on my behalf to provide this service. You can read Formspree's privacy policy.

I may also use my email provider and other essential business technology providers to receive, store and respond to messages. I do not sell personal information.

Some service providers may process or store information outside the UK. Where this happens, I will take account of the applicable UK data-protection requirements and appropriate safeguards.

How long I keep enquiries

For general enquiries that do not become client work, I normally keep the correspondence for up to 12 months after our last communication, then delete it when it is no longer needed.

If you become a client, I may keep relevant business records for longer where needed to provide services, manage contractual matters or comply with accounting and legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access the personal information I hold about you, correct inaccurate information, request deletion or restriction of its use, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Some rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To make a request, email tiffanybale@hotmail.co.uk. If you have concerns, please contact me first so I can try to resolve them. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator.

Website technology

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, technical information such as IP addresses may be processed to deliver the website and keep it secure. I do not currently use the enquiry information you submit for automated decision-making or profiling.

Changes to this notice

I'll update this notice if the way I use personal information or the services supporting this website change. The latest version will be available on this page.
